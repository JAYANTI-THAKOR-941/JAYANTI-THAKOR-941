## About

I work at the intersection of cybersecurity, software development and technical education. My focus is on understanding how systems work, building reliable applications and translating complex technical concepts into practical learning experiences.

I develop full-stack applications, create networking and security labs, and mentor students through hands-on technical projects. Through TechSkillHub and GenZHackars, I aim to make technology education and digital safety more accessible.
